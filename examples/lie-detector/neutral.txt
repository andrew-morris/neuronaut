# Everyday sentences with nothing deceptive in them.
The train to the coast leaves at nine every morning.
She watered the tomatoes and closed the greenhouse door.
The library added a new shelf of travel guides.
He bought bread, eggs and a bag of oranges.
Our neighbours painted their fence a pale green.
The meeting was moved to the small room on the second floor.
The kettle clicked off and she poured the tea.
A light rain fell over the harbour all afternoon.
The bus stopped outside the post office.
He folded the map and put it in the glovebox.
The children built a sandcastle near the pier.
The bakery opens early on Saturdays.
