# True statements settled with but/though/while: the conjunction control.
She told him the check was in the mail, but it might take a week to arrive.
The dealer said the car had never been in an accident, though it did need new tyres.
The boy said he had brushed his teeth, but he had forgotten to floss.
The landlord said the flat had no damp, though the windows let in a draught.
The waiter said the fish was caught that morning, but the oysters were from yesterday.
He told the bank his business was profitable, though margins had been thin that year.
The guide said the path was safe, but steep for the last mile.
The tenant said the rent was in the post, while the deposit would follow next week.
He told the officer he had only had one beer, though he had been at the bar for an hour.
The seller said the watch was solid gold, but the strap was leather.
